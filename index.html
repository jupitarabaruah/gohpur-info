About Gohpur

Gohpur is on north bank of Brahmaputra. Known as City of Peace. 35km from Biswanath Chariali, 50km from Tezpur. Population mixed - Assamese, Bodo, Nepali, Bengali live together.

Food & Culture

Food: Khar, Masor Tenga, Pitha, Tea. Festival: Bihu, Durga Puja, Ali-Aye Ligang, Bodo Festival. People very friendly and peaceful.

How to Reach Gohpur

Train: Gohpur Railway Station (GHU)
Bus: From Guwahati, Tezpur, Lakhimpur
Airport: Tezpur (50km), Lakhimpur Lilabari (90km)
